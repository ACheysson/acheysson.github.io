About me
======

I am a research analyst at the Joint Research Centre of the European Commission in Ispra, where I am part of the Data and Platform Economy and Regulation Team studying digital industrial organization.

My research spans theoretical and structural industrial organization, the economics of migration, and political science. Recently I've been narrowing my focus to industrial organization, but I'm still passionate about my previous research areas!

I got my Ph.D. at the European University Institute in 2024, was a research fellow at the University of Bologna, and then joined the European Commission.
