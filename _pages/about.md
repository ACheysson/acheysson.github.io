About me
======

I am a researcher at ZEW – Leibniz Centre for European Economic Research in Mannheim, in the Digital Economy unit. I work on industrial organization, with a focus on how consumers’ simultaneous use of several platforms and services (multi-homing) shapes competition in digital markets. I use structural and theoretical models to study media, videogames, data markets and the airline industry.

I also work on questions beyond IO, including the economics of migration, gender quotas in the European Parliament, and education policy. These are areas I remain passionate about.

I received my Ph.D. from the European University Institute in 2024. I was then a research fellow at the University of Bologna and a research analyst at the Joint Research Centre of the European Commission, before joining ZEW in October 2026.
